1 · Turn on the microphone

Tiny Tunes listens to his singing so it can show him which note he sang. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

2 · Find Sa

Sa is the home note, and in Indian music anyone can choose it. Let him sing one long, comfortable note (not a rhyme), or sing a long Saaaa yourself. When the note below stays steady, tap Use this as Sa.

3 · The singing voice

The lessons are sung by a real man's voice (Sa, Re, Ma and Dha, from a free recording). Other notes play on harmonium until someone records a voice (a lesson uses the recorded voice once all its notes are recorded).

How to sit with him: during Listen, hum along softly. During Your turn, stay quiet, point at the glowing circle and wait: the app can't tell your voice from his.

Best: record a family member's voice, yours or a grandparent's. You don't need to sing well: just say each note long, like Saaa, and the app tunes it to the right note. It takes about 2 minutes. Children love hearing a voice they know.
