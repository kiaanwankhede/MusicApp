Why this approach

Designed around how he learns

🔁

He echoes → we teach by echo

Indian classical is taught guru–shishya: the teacher sings a phrase, the student sings it back. His echolalia makes this natural for him.

🧑‍🏫

He copies exactly → real voice only

No computer voices. Every phrase he hears is a real singer, in tune, at his Sa. The app checks each recording before he ever hears it.

⭐

He loves rhymes → rhymes lead to sargam

Twinkle Twinkle is Sa Sa Pa Pa Dha Dha Pa · Ma Ma Ga Ga Re Re Sa. A tune he knows, with new note names.

🪔

Loud sounds excite him → everything calm

Volume limit, soft tanpura, no clapping or cheering sounds, a glowing diya instead of fireworks, and a बस (stop) button always on screen.

LEVEL 1

Sa ka gharसा का घर

Find Sa, the home note, and hold it with the tanpura for 2–3 seconds.

In a session

  1. The tanpura fades in softly at his Sa.
  2. The teacher's clip sings "mmm…" then "Saaa…"; the Sa step lights up.
  3. His turn: a small diya moves with his voice and glows when he is on Sa.
  4. 3–5 tries, then on to the next part of the riyaz.

On screen

  • Only one step, Sa, so there is nothing else to think about.
  • No score and no "wrong" sound; close is celebrated with a softer glow.
Move on whenHe hums or sings Sa with the drone most days.
Parent tipHum along with him. If he gets excited and loud, sing softer; he copies volume too.
Riyaz ki Shuruaat · Tiny Tunes v2 design draft5
LEVEL 2

Echoसुनो और गाओ

Sing back short 2–3 note phrases, the guru–shishya way.

In a session

  1. The teacher sings a phrase; each note's step lights as it is sung.
  2. His turn: dots trace his notes on the steps; each matched note glows.
  3. Up to 8 phrases, starting small: Sa Re Sa · Sa Re Ga · Ga Re Sa · Sa Pa Sa

On screen

  • Only the notes in today's phrases are shown (Sa, Re, Ga at first).
  • "सुनो" (listen) and "अब तुम" (your turn) always show whose turn it is.
Move on whenHe echoes most phrases close to pitch.
Parent tipHis echolalia helps here. Let him repeat a phrase as often as he likes; if he sings it later in the day, sing it back.

LEVEL 3

Rhymes in sargamकविता में सरगम

Learn the note names through a rhyme he already loves.

In a session

  1. The teacher sings one line with words: "Twinkle twinkle little star".
  2. The same line in sargam: Sa Sa Pa Pa Dha Dha Pa.
  3. He echoes the sargam line; each note chip lights as he sings it.
  4. Next line: Ma Ma Ga Ga Re Re Sa. Later, the whole rhyme together.

On screen

  • A picture for the rhyme, the words in small text, and big coloured note chips.
  • One line at a time (6 lines in Twinkle).
Move on whenHe sings a line on his own.
Parent tipAdd his other favourite rhymes next. His teacher can write their sargam, and you record them in the app.

LEVEL 4

Aaroh–avarohआरोह–अवरोह

Go up and down the scale in tune, slowly, then simple alankars.

In a session

  1. Aaroh Sa Re Ga Ma Pa, then avaroh Pa Ma Ga Re Sa; he echoes each.
  2. Alankars in pairs: SaRe ReGa GaMa MaPa, then threes: SaReGa ReGaMa GaMaPa.
  3. Dha, Ni and upper Sa are added only when his voice is ready.

On screen

  • The full ladder Sa → Pa; the title says "going up" or "coming down".
  • About one note per second; the app never speeds up by itself.
Move on whenSa → Pa → Sa is in tune at slow speed.
Parent tipSlow is correct. Show "up" and "down" with your hand as he sings.

LEVEL 5 · ALONGSIDE LEVELS 2–4

Taalताल

Keep a steady beat, then Keherwa (8 beats) and Dadra (6 beats).

In a session (1 minute a day)

  1. Clap steadily with a slow, soft beat; no counting yet.
  2. Keherwa: clap on 1 (taali), wave on 5 (khaali), counting 1–8.
  3. Dadra: clap on 1, wave on 4, counting 1–6.
  4. Later, the bols: Dha Ge Na Ti | Na Ka Dhi Na.

On screen

  • Beat circles light up in turn; a hand picture shows clap 👏 or wave 👋.
  • A quiet beat; no sound effects for a missed clap.
Move on whenHe claps steadily with the beat.
Parent tipStart by patting his knees together to the beat; touch helps rhythm. Keep it quiet.

LEVEL 6

First raagराग भूपाली

A first taste of a real raag. Bhoopali uses only 5 notes: Sa Re Ga Pa Dha.

In a session

  1. Aaroh Sa Re Ga Pa Dha and back, by echo (no Ma, no Ni).
  2. Short raag phrases: Sa Re Ga · Ga Re Sa · Ga Pa Dha Pa · Dha Pa Ga Re Sa.
  3. Upper Sa joins when his voice is ready.

On screen

  • The ladder shows Ma and Ni greyed out: "not in this raag".
  • The same echo game as Level 2, with raag phrases.
ThenHe is ready for a guru's beginner (Prarambhik-level) classes.
Parent tipHis teacher may prefer another first raag, such as Yaman. The app works with whatever notes they record.

FOR PARENTS AND TEACHER

Grown-ups corner🔒

Opened by holding the lock for 2 seconds, so he can't change settings by accident.

Recording studio

  • About 30 short clips, recorded once by his teacher or a parent.
  • The tanpura plays while recording; the app checks the pitch of every clip and asks for a retake if a note is off, so he only ever copies an in-tune voice.

Settings

  • Sa pitch: starts at D (safed 2); his teacher can change it.
  • Volume limit, tanpura level, slow speed, which levels are open.

Today's log

  • What he did today (Sa, echoes, taal, minutes), to share with his teacher.

Privacy

  • Recordings stay on the phone. No ads, no account, works without internet.
