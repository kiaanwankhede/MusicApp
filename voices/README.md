# Voices

`sa re ga ma pa dha ni sa2 (.mp3)` are the sounds played for Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa' (C D E F G A B C).

Each is the open **Kokoro-82M** neural voice (Apache-2.0, Hindi female voice `hf_alpha`) saying the syllable, over a soft harmonium note at the exact pitch. `sa2` is the upper Sa.

To use a real singer, replace these files (same names; .mp3/.m4a/.wav/.ogg/.webm all work) and push; the APK rebuilds automatically. Parents can also record their own voice per note in the app (Grown-ups area), which overrides these on that device.
